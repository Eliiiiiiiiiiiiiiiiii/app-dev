# 🎮 *Alice in Borderland*

> *"In a world where games decide who lives and who dies, survival is the ultimate challenge."*

---

## 📖 Overview

**Alice in Borderland** (Japanese: 今際の国のアリス, *Imawa no Kuni no Arisu*) is a Japanese **sci-fi thriller series** based on the manga by **Haro Aso**.  
It follows a group of young people trapped in a mysterious, deserted Tokyo, where they must play **deadly games** to survive.

---

---

## 🎭 Main Characters

### 🔹 **Ryohei Arisu (有栖 良平)**
- A clever but aimless young man obsessed with video games  
- Uses his strategic mind to survive the *Borderland* challenges  

### 🔹 **Yuzuha Usagi (宇佐木 柚葉)**
- A strong and independent mountain climber  
- Becomes Arisu’s trusted companion and love interest  

### 🔹 **Shuntarō Chishiya (苣屋 俊)**
- Calm, cunning, and mysterious  
- Prefers strategy over brute strength  

---

## 🕹️ Themes & Symbolism

- **Survival & Morality** → How far will humans go to stay alive?  
- **Society & Isolation** → The *Borderland* reflects the emptiness of modern life  
- **Identity & Growth** → Arisu’s journey mirrors the struggle for meaning  

> 💬 “The games aren’t about winning. They’re about understanding what it means to live.”

<summary>🧩 <strong>Plot Summary</strong> (click to expand)</summary>

Ryohei Arisu, a disillusioned gamer, suddenly finds himself in a deserted Tokyo with his two best friends.  
They are forced to participate in a series of high-stakes games that test their **intelligence, teamwork, and morality**.  

As Arisu loses people he cares about, he forms a bond with **Yuzuha Usagi**, and together they uncover shocking truths about the world they’re trapped in — the *Borderland* may not be what it seems.

## 🧠 Fun Fact

The **name “Arisu”** is the Japanese pronunciation of **“Alice,”** referencing *Alice in Wonderland* — another story about entering a strange new world.
